Sharing food locations

An agent that touches food remembers where it is and tells the agents it passes. They pass the news on, a little staler each time. Agents that have been told head for the food. Once they eat they are full for a while: they wander off to explore and spread the news, but don't follow news themselves. Explorers never follow news, so the colony keeps searching for new food.
