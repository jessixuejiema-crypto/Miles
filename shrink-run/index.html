Shrink Run

Reach the flag. Each time you finish, you shrink by 1.5×: smaller body, slower run, lower jump.

Watch out for spinning bars: touching one sends you back to the start. Striped platforms are one-way, so you can jump up through them. Some blocks have tunnels inside.

Before every new size the level tests itself. If the smaller you can't finish, it adds stairs and ledges or cuts into blocks until every size you've been can still make it.

Move: ← → or A D · Jump: ↑ W or Space · Restart: R
Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, Recenter (C) to follow your player.
AI play (I) lets the computer play and keep shrinking. After all 7 sizes, a harder level starts, forever.
