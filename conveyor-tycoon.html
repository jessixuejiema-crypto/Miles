How to play

  • Tap the $ coin (top-left) to open the shop. Pick a thing, then click / drag on the ground to place it.
  • R or the ↻ button rotates before placing. Dragging conveyors auto-turns them along your path.
  • Right-click (or the 🧹 broom) removes a machine for a 50% refund — and any items it was carrying are swept into your 🎒 inventory, where you can sell them.
  • The 🌳 button shows the whole recipe tree: what turns into what, with which machine, and the profit of each step.
  • The hand ✋ pans the map. Mouse wheel or +/− zooms. Click a placed Item Cutter with the hand to switch it between slabs / stairs / fences. Click a fully grown tree to hand-harvest it for quick cash.
  • Layers: a Lift Conveyor lifts items exactly one layer up, straight up. Go up a layer (▲ button) and you'll see a dashed blue landing box on the same tile: put a conveyor there to catch them. Down Conveyors bring items one layer down the same way, with a purple landing box below, and show as a ghost from underneath so you always see both ends. R turns them to line up with your belts. Chain lift → belt → lift to reach layer 3+.
  • Tree logic: a Tree Detector lights up next to a grown tree. A Tree Cutter beside the same tree then chops it automatically — no wire needed for that simple rig. Wires, levers and clocks can also power a cutter from farther away. The wood drops on the belt the cutter faces (or any belt beside it), the tree regrows, and the cutter shows a ⚡ badge whenever it has no signal. The Tree Farm is the same rig, pre-packaged.

Logic & power

  • Wire carries power. Lever: click to switch on/off. Clock: its hand turns once every 10 s and fires a 1 s pulse each full turn.
  • Gates read their two SIDE tiles (the NOT gate reads the tile BEHIND instead) and light the wire at their FRONT — the little gold arrow shows which way the front is, and the pip glows when it's on. AND: both sides on. OR: any side on. XOR: sides different. XNOR: sides matching. NAND: not both. NOR: all quiet. NOT: the opposite of behind. Click any placed gate with the hand and the info bar explains it and shows whether it's ON right now.
  • The Ore Mine only runs while powered — park a lever beside it, wire it to a detector, or pulse it with a clock.
