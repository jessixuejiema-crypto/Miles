Ants start out driving completely at random. Smack into a wall and it's back to the nest — and at first the colony shrugs it off like nothing happened. But each crash leaves the tiniest dent in the shared memory, and after smacking into the same walls hundreds of times the lesson very slowly sinks in: steering steadies, crash-prone routes fade, and trips that reach the food lock in the good ones. Patience — or crank the speed slider to fast-forward through driving school.
