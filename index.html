Rules
  • Predators chase the nearest prey they can see; prey flee from any predator in range.
  • A touch eats the prey and the hunter immediately spawns 2–3 cubs.
  • 30s without a meal and a predator starves into nothing.
  • Prey that survive 10–20s split off 2 offspring.
  • The world wraps at the edges; crowding past the population cap suppresses births.
