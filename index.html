Every ball is a magnetic dipole, not a monopole: it has a north half and a south half, it is free to spin, and it feels the field of every other ball. Because of that the classic behaviour falls out on its own — dipoles attract end-to-end and repel side-by-side, so they snap into chains and then curl those chains into rings. Nothing choreographs that; it is just the force law.

The force falls off as 1/r⁴, which is brutally short-ranged: balls more than a couple of diameters apart barely feel each other, so a spread-out Scatter mostly sits there in pairs. That is honest physics, not a bug — use Attract to sweep them together and watch the structures build. The uniform Field applies torque but no net force, so it turns every ball like a compass needle without dragging it anywhere.
