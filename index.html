How to play

On your turn, either step your pawn one square (tap a dot — you can jump over the enemy pawn), or place a wall by tapping a groove between squares. Walls are two squares long, can't cross other walls, and may never seal off a player's last way through. Blue wins by reaching the top row; Red wins at the bottom row.
