How it works
  • Each generator's ring fills around its edge. When the ring closes, a ball drops into the marble run. The mini generator beside it drops a smaller ball a bit faster.
  • Balls only pay c when they touch a coloured hazard. Red pays ×1, orange ×2, yellow ×4, green ×8 and so on; each colour is twice as rare as the one before. Deeper mazes allow rarer colours.
  • Each maze is a labyrinth of corridors seen from the side. Floors tilt toward drop shafts; where a floor peaks between two shafts the path forks. Shafts hold spinners, bouncers, moving gates and pegs. Some corridors are dead ends that tip balls into the recycle chute, where a fan or ladder carries them back to the top.
  • The only way out is a shuttered pit near the high end of the bottom floor. Balls that miss it roll over a shuttered hazard pit on their way back to the chute.
  • When a ball breaks into an unexplored maze, the next one appears beside it or below it, forever. Sizes are shown in ub (1 ub = one ball width).
  • A ball that hasn't moved for 10 to 40 seconds is cleared.
  • Drag in any direction to look around and pinch with two fingers to zoom (trackpad pinch or the − / + buttons on a computer). Earn c to level up and spend skill points in the Skill Tree.
