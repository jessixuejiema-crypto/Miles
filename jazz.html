Inside the model

The chord changes come from a second-order Markov chain trained on the standards below: it counts which chord follows each pair of chords, then samples new progressions from those odds (blended with first-order odds and a temperature set by Creativity). Song-form rules keep the cadences in place. The soloist is a probabilistic note sampler. It scores each candidate pitch for chord-tone fit, smooth voice leading, bebop approach notes and phrase shape, then samples one. It also reuses its own motifs.
