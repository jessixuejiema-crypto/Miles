How it works. Each AI is a small neural network (9 inputs → 10 hidden → 2 outputs). It sees where the ball is on the platform, how fast it moves, the platform's tilt and its own position on the track. It outputs how hard to roll left/right and how fast to tilt the platform. The ball has no friction, so it slides whenever the platform tilts, and every bounce is perfectly elastic: the ball never loses speed. Every AI is tested on 3 drops; fitness = seconds the ball stayed up, plus a bonus for keeping it near the centre. The top AIs survive unchanged, and the rest of the next generation is made by mixing the weights of two good parents and adding small random mutations.
