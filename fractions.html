Hover (or tap) a cell to see its simplified form — its whole family of equivalent fractions lights up along a ray from the origin. Click to pin a family. Press Merge all to watch every yellow fraction slide onto its lowest-terms form, leaving its cell blank — the surviving cells then recolor by crowding: the more fractions stacked onto a cell, the stronger its color. As the grid grows, the share of blue cells approaches 6/π² ≈ 60.8%.
