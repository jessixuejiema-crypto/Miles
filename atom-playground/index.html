    ⚛ How the Atom Playground works

    Everything in the universe is built from a few elementary particles. Drop them in and see what they make!

    • Quarks pull on each other. Three quarks make a baryon: u u d = proton, u d d = neutron. A quark + antiquark make a short-lived meson (pion, kaon…).
    • Protons + neutrons stick together (strong force) to form a nucleus. The number of protons decides the element.
    • Add electrons to a nucleus until it's neutral → a real atom.
    • Neutral atoms with free bonds snap into molecules: H₂O, CO₂, NaCl, CH₄, NH₃…
    • Atoms have electron clouds that keep them apart. Throw them hard (or turn up the heat) to fuse them into heavier elements. Bare nuclei fuse on touch.
    • Wrong number of neutrons? The nucleus is radioactive ☢ and will β⁻, β⁺ or α decay. Normally this is sped up so you can watch it. ☢ Real decay time uses each isotope's real half-life (uranium-235 takes 704 million years!). ⏱ Set decay time makes every radioactive atom decay after the number of seconds you type.
    • Fire a neutron at Uranium-235 for fission and a chain reaction.
    • Antimatter (positrons, antiquarks, antiprotons) annihilates with matter into gamma rays.
    • Photons make atoms glow (fluorescence). Gamma rays knock electrons out or make electron–positron pairs.
    • Turn up 🪐 Gravity and atoms pull on each other and clump into asteroids, planets, brown dwarfs and (with 1,000+ hydrogen atoms) stars. Tick ⚖ Real mass gravity to make each particle pull by its true mass. Try the Planet maker and Star maker experiments.
    • The 🌡 Heat slider is a real temperature. Each element and molecule is solid, liquid or gas depending on its real melting and boiling points. Tick ⬇ Ground gravity to make things fall to the floor.
    • Light has exact wavelengths. Pick a photon's wavelength with the λ slider. Atoms only absorb their own exact wavelengths, so you'll see the electron jump up and fall back, giving out the element's fingerprint colours (hydrogen's red line is 656.3 nm). The 🌈 Spectrometer records them.
    • W bosons turn neutrons into protons (and back). Z and Higgs bosons decay fast — the Higgs field slows things nearby.
    • Heavy quarks, muons and taus decay into lighter particles. Neutrinos are ghosts that fly through everything.

    Controls: pick a particle, then click to drop, drag to launch, hold still to spray. ✋ Grab lets you fling things. ⚡ Zap blasts energy. 🎲 Scatter drops lots of the selected particle at random spots. 🖐 Pan moves your view; the scroll wheel or a two-finger pinch zooms. 🧱 Border makes the box walls bouncy, 🌀 Wrap sends things out one side and back in the other, and with both off it's open space. 📏 Box sets how wide the box is, from 10 atoms up to 1,000,000 atoms.
