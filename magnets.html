The map
Every point on the map is simulated: a ball is dropped there, left to run until it stops, and the point is painted the color of the magnet it landed on. So a ball you spawn on yellow ground really does end up on the yellow magnet. Black means it never reached one. The map is recomputed whenever you add, move or change a magnet.
