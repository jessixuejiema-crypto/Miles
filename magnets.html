Color rule
A ball takes the color of the ground it spawns on. Every magnet pulls it, but only a magnet of its own color can hold it — a wrong-colored one whips it straight back out. So a ball born on yellow wanders the field and always ends up parked on a yellow magnet. Nothing blocks it on the way: the colors are pull, not walls. A ball born on black is neutral and never moves.
