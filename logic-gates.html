Logic Bench

Drop gates on the board, wire them together and watch the signals light up. High signals glow green, low signals stay grey.

Click a chip
Clicking a part in the left bar shows what it does and its truth table, and loads it ready to drop — then click anywhere on the board to place one. Click the chip again to put it down.
Drag a chip
Or drag a part straight from the bar onto the spot you want it.
Inputs
AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR and XNOR take two to six inputs. Use the − and + next to Inputs in the panel — on a chip before you place it, or on a gate already on the board. The truth table follows along.
Wire tool
Drag from an output port to an input port. Click-then-click works too. An input accepts several wires and behaves like a wired OR.
Cut tool
Slash a line across every wire you want gone. A plain click cuts just the wire under the cursor.
Click a switch
With the Select tool, click a switch to flip it. Buttons stay high only while you hold them down.
Latches
SR and D latches are level driven; the D, T and JK flip-flops trigger on a rising clock edge. Cross-coupled NOR gates latch on their own too — see the examples menu.
